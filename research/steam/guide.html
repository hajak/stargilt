Gate 0 · The one decision everything hangs on

Paid, low tier, + free demorecommended

  • e.g. $4.99 — recoups the $100 at ≈200 copies
  • Demo = its own free App ID, no second fee
  • Keeps Steam Next Fest meaningful (needs a demo)
  • Can go paid→free later, cleanly

Free

  • $100 is sunk forever (recoup needs $1,000 revenue)
  • No payout/Swedish-tax questions at all
  • One-way-ish door: free→paid later = support ticket, 1 week public notice, and every free claimer keeps it forever
Also decide now, because they freeze later: the exact name (“StarGilt — Forged in Chains”? — frozen at page review), and launch platforms (recommended: Windows + native Linux; macOS deferred — it's the only platform needing a certificate: Apple notarization, $99/yr).
